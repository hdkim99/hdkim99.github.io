RoutineMatch privacy policy

Effective: 20 November 2026

Information stored on your device

No account is required. Routine names, steps, notes, repeat schedules and run history are stored on your device. We do not send this content to a developer server or use it for ad targeting. The next schedule for the widget is stored in this app's shared container. Automatic synchronization between devices is not provided.

Notifications and files

We ask for notification permission when you set up notifications. Core features work if you decline. You choose where to store exported backups. Backup files are not separately encrypted; keep them in a safe location.

Advertising and privacy choices

The app uses Google Mobile Ads 13.13.0 and User Messaging Platform 3.1.0. Limited ads are enabled; personalized ads, publisher first-party identifiers and SDK crash reporting are disabled. The app does not call ATT or IDFA APIs. Ads are hidden during running and paused routines.

No production ad request is made when the age group is unspecified or under 18. Your age-group choice is saved only on this device for this app; we do not ask for a date of birth. Before requesting production ads, the app updates UMP consent information and presents Google's forms where required. When available, reopen privacy choices in Settings to change or withdraw your choices. Applicable requirements are determined through the SDK, not from the app language or App Store country.

Google and advertising partners may process IP addresses, approximate location, device and app information, ad impressions and interactions, performance and diagnostic information, and related storage for ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Depending on the account's programmatic limited-ads setting, cookies or local storage may be used to detect invalid traffic. Limited ads do not mean no data collection. Google's policies describe its retention and international processing practices.

How Google uses data from partner apps · Google privacy policy

Retention and deletion

Local data is retained until you delete it. Delete All Data in Settings removes routines, the active run and history, and clears associated notifications and Live Activities. Exported copies must be deleted separately from their chosen location. Deleting app data also resets the app's advertising age-group choice.

Support and this website

You choose whether to send a support email. Your sender address and the information or attachments you provide are used to address your request; unnecessary material is deleted after resolution. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information under its own privacy policy.
