I cannot hear a notification

Check notification permission, Focus mode and volume in device settings. Use Reschedule Notifications in the app's Settings to schedule upcoming events again. Notification delivery, sound and appearance depend on system settings.

Time has passed since I last opened the app

Locking the screen or closing the app does not pause a routine. A step is never completed automatically when its timer expires. Complete the step yourself or use Edit Time Left to specify how much longer the current step needs.

Backups and restore

Export a backup from Settings. End the current run before importing a file, then choose Merge or Replace. Back up existing data before replacing it. An active run and advertising choices are not imported from a backup.

My saved data will not open

Do not uninstall the app. Save a copy of the original data file from Settings and contact support. You do not need to send private routine information or the file contents without being asked.

Privacy choices

When required by the advertising SDK, Settings offers an entry to reopen advertising privacy choices. Declining, changing your choices, or an unavailable ad does not block routines, history or backups.

What to include in a support request

Please include the device model, OS version, app version, the action that caused the issue and the error message. Do not send passwords. Support pages are available in Korean and English.
